# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Browser users working on ordinary web pages who open the Chrome/Edge side panel (or floating assistant) for short, task-bound sessions. They switch tabs often, need the assistant to see the current page without leaving the page, and must keep chatting when models, tools, network extraction, or debugger automation fail or are turned off.

## Product Purpose

The AI sidebar is the Browser AI Assistant surface of the Moon Tab MV3 extension. It owns conversation, model selection, page-context injection, tool calling, browser-control preferences, history, and settings. Success means: the empty state and composer are immediately usable; page sharing is explicit and dismissible; high-risk browser automation stays opt-in with clear status; and configuration (API keys, models, tools) is reachable without burying the primary chat task.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, restrained. The panel should feel like a quiet tool rail that disappears into the conversation—familiar Chrome/Gemini side-panel grammar, not decorative new-tab warmth or game-page playfulness.

## Anti-references

Avoid dense AI choice surfaces, color-only focus or selection states, CJK labels forced into uppercase tracking, low-contrast muted body text, cream/warm-paper canvas tints, and decorative glass or second accents. Do not let tool menus, automation modes, or settings chrome crowd out the conversation. Do not treat the new-tab moon/pet language or the game surface as the default identity of this panel.

## Design Principles

1. Conversation first: empty-state copy and the composer lead; chrome stays quiet until needed.
2. One primary action per decision point: send, choose model, share a tab, or authorize a boundary—never a wall of equal controls.
3. Trust through explicit state: shared tabs, browser-control on/off, automation mode, tool calling, and errors must read as labels and status, not color alone.
4. Narrow-rail craft: fixed rem type, 2.25rem control grid, popovers that escape overflow, no fluid display type.
5. CJK-native labels: sentence case, letter-spacing 0, no Latin UI tropes applied to Chinese copy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for body text, placeholders, and focus-visible rings (3px blue at ~24% on white, 2px offset). Preserve keyboard operation for menus, drawers, and dialogs; pair selection with checks or labels, not color alone; honor `prefers-reduced-motion` for spins, pulses, and drawer transitions; keep primary interactive controls on a ≥2.25rem grid and touch-friendly where the floating panel is used on smaller viewports.
